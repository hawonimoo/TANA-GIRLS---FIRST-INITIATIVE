ABOUT US

Who We Are

Empowering Girls. Building Voices.

Tana Girls First Initiative was founded in April 2026 with a commitment to supporting girls and creating awareness around challenges that affect their lives.

Our work focuses on mentorship, education encouragement, girls' rights awareness, confidence building and community engagement.

We create safe opportunities for girls to ask questions, learn about their rights and develop the confidence to speak for themselves.

OUR FOUNDER

The Story Behind the Initiative

"Every girl deserves to have a voice, to be heard and to believe in her own future."

Tana Girls First Initiative was inspired by a personal journey and a desire to stand with girls facing challenges that can make it difficult for them to speak for themselves.

The initiative was created to provide girls with mentorship, awareness and encouragement while helping them understand their rights and recognize their potential.

Through this work, we hope to create a community where girls feel confident enough to speak, learn and make informed decisions about their futures.
