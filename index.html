ABOUT US

Who We Are & What We Stand For

Empowering Girls. Safeguarding Dignity.

Tana Girls First Initiative CBO is a registered Community-Based Organisation established to promote the rights, dignity, protection, and empowerment of girls across Tana County, Kenya. Registered on 21 October 2024, our organisation commenced active community programmes in 2026.

Our work addresses deep-rooted challenges affecting girls, including barriers to education, inadequate access to menstrual health supplies, Gender-Based Violence (GBV), Female Genital Mutilation (FGM), early and forced marriage, discrimination, and limited opportunities for mentorship.

We also recognize the severe pressures of climate change on vulnerable households. Recurrent drought, water scarcity, and flooding in Tana County severely disrupt girls' education, safety, and health. Girls need safe environments, access to essential resources, and resilient communities.

A Climate-Resilient, Empowered Society

A just, inclusive, and climate-resilient society where every girl is safe, empowered, educated, and free from violence, harmful practices, discrimination, and the unequal effects of climate change.

Protection, Education & Empowerment

To empower and protect girls through education, advocacy, mentorship, menstrual health support, climate change awareness, and community engagement, while working to eliminate Gender-Based Violence, Female Genital Mutilation, early marriage, and other harmful practices.

WHAT WE DO

Our Core Programme Areas

Addressing the root challenges facing girls across Tana County through targeted, community-driven interventions.

📚

Girls' Education & School Retention

Promoting girls' access to education, tackling barriers that cause dropouts, conducting school retention visits, and engaging teachers and families to keep girls enrolled and completing secondary school.

🌸

Menstrual Health & Hygiene

Supporting menstrual dignity by distributing sanitary pads, eradicating period poverty, breaking cultural taboos, and ensuring that no girl misses valuable school days due to lack of hygiene supplies.

🛡️

Girls' Rights & Child Protection

Creating widespread awareness on constitutional rights, advocating fiercely against Gender-Based Violence (GBV), Female Genital Mutilation (FGM), and early forced marriage, while building safe community referral pathways.

🎓

Mentorship & Leadership Development

Connecting girls with positive female role models to build self-confidence, communication, goal-setting, and decision-making skills that prepare them for future leadership in society.

🌍

Climate Change Awareness & Resilience

Educating girls and communities on climate crises — including recurring drought, water scarcity, and flooding in Tana County — and empowering young women to take an active role in tree planting and environmental conservation.

🤝

Community Partnerships & Advocacy

Mobilizing parents, village elders, community volunteers, and institutional partners to create an inclusive, sustainable ecosystem that protects and champions girls' wellbeing.

OUR FOUNDER

The Story Behind the Vision

"Every girl deserves to have a voice, to be heard, to be protected, and to believe in the strength of her own future."

Tana Girls First Initiative CBO was inspired by a personal journey and a relentless determination to stand with girls confronting socio-cultural challenges that silence their potential.

Having observed firsthand the impacts of school dropouts, period poverty, child marriage, and the acute pressures brought by climate-related droughts and floods in Tana River County, I recognized that girls need more than encouragement. They need safe spaces, protection, resources, and a supportive community.

We established this CBO on 21 October 2024 to be a beacon of dignity and justice — a platform where every girl discovers the power of her own voice and receives the tools to lead.

Hawo Nimoo Farah

Founder & CEO — Tana Girls First Initiative CBO
